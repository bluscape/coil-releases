Flashing and debugging an ESP32-C3 right after it's plugged in works again. In 0.2.2 and earlier it could fail with "board … was reset or unplugged; this connection is gone (… endpoint STALL condition)".

**Tested with:** ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C6 over the chip's built-in USB-JTAG, and the classic ESP32 through an ESP-Prog. Windows 10 and 11 (x64).

## What's new

- **ESP32-C3 just plugged in or fully reset:** the first reset that stops the chip for flashing makes its USB-JTAG report one error, and then works. Coil treated that error as fatal and fell back to a full chip reset, which brought the error back each time, so the flash or debug session failed. Coil now tries that reset a second time first. This affects VS Code (Flash and Debug), Espressif-IDE and `coil-flash`.
