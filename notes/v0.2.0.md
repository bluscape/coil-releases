Coil now debugs and flashes the **ESP32-C3**, over the chip's built-in USB-JTAG, in VS Code, Espressif-IDE and the terminal.

**Tested with:** ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C6 over the chip's built-in USB-JTAG, and the classic ESP32 through an ESP-Prog. Windows 10 and 11 (x64).

## What's new

- **ESP32-C3:** debugging (breakpoints in flash and RAM, stepping, variables, watchpoints; reset to `app_main` in about a second), flashing over JTAG (only the 64 KB chunks that changed; blank chips too) and over serial, and the serial monitor.
- **Watchpoints in GDB on the ESP32-C6 and C3** now show the old and new value (they stopped with a bare SIGTRAP before).
- The installer no longer reports a failed ESP-IDF install when ESP-IDF did install (EIM couldn't save its settings file).
- The programs carry the release version in their Windows version information.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Coil-Setup-0.2.0.exe` | **Espressif-IDE.** Installs Coil per user and offers Espressif-IDE, ESP-IDF (through Espressif's installer, EIM) and the USB drivers when they're missing or out of date. Optional weekly update check. Run it over 0.1.0 to update. |
| `coil-0.2.0.vsix` | **VS Code**, until Coil is on the Marketplace: **Extensions → … → Install from VSIX**. Its Setup view installs ESP-IDF, the drivers and code completion. |

After installing for Espressif-IDE, set your debug configuration's OpenOCD **Executable path** to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Coil\bin\coil-gdb.exe`. The [guide](https://coil.zone/guide/#espressif-ide) has the steps.
